# Schritt 5: Deine Prüfung

This step is you, not a subagent. The orchestrator shows you `mca summary` and this checklist, then asks for a decision.

## Checkliste
1. **Vergleichstabelle:** Is any value implausible? Are the gaps ("LÜCKE") acceptable, or do they need another source?
2. **Konflikte:** For each conflict, do you agree with the chosen fact and the reason given?
3. **Stichprobe:** Open two facts at their source (`source_id` + locator) and check that the value and the quote match.
4. **Hinweise:** Is each outlier real or a misread? Should any mismatched unit or period be pulled in with a new source?
5. **Kernaussagen:** For each one: is it true, relevant and backed by the cited facts? Does the headline state the "so what"?
6. **Diagramme:** Are they readable, and do they show the right KPI?

## Entscheidung
- **Freigegeben:** step 6 exports Excel and PowerPoint.
- **Änderungen:** give concrete comments, one per line, that name the ID they concern, for example `K1: Bericht S2 bevorzugen, Sondereffekt ist bereinigt`. Steps 3 and 4 then run again. There are at most 3 revision rounds.
